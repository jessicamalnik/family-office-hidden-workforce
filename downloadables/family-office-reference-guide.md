# Family office workforce reference guide

## What a family office is

A private organization that manages the financial and personal infrastructure of one wealthy family.

## Core departments

- Investments.
- Accounting.
- Tax.
- Legal.
- Executive operations.
- Property.
- Travel.
- Security.
- Philanthropy.
- Technology.
- Cybersecurity.
- Governance.
- Succession.
- Next-generation preparation.

## Common roles

- Chief investment officer.
- Investment analyst.
- Controller.
- Tax director.
- Chief of staff.
- Property manager.
- Security professional.
- Philanthropy officer.
- Technology professional.
- Family-governance specialist.

## Common career proving grounds

- Goldman Sachs.
- Morgan Stanley.
- Big Four accounting firms.
- Private equity.
- Investment banking.
- Private banking.
- Estate law.

## Central operating principle

Trust is more important than public visibility.

Family-office hiring often relies on existing relationships, referrals, discretion, and reputation.
