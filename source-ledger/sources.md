## Documentary analysis

These points are arguments made by the documentary rather than externally verified findings:

- Family-office hiring operates as a hidden relationship economy.
- Reputation can travel faster than résumés.
- Introductions can matter more than applications.
- Family offices can resemble small private companies built around one family.
- Money is only one department inside a mature family office.
- Trust is the central operating principle of the industry.
- The cost of a bad hire is unusually high because employees may gain access to investments, taxes, legal documents, homes, travel, security, and family members.
